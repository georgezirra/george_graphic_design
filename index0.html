About Me

PASSION FOR DESIGN

I'm George Zirra, a creative graphic designer with a keen eye for visual storytelling. I operate under the brand George Best Designs, bringing ideas to life through purposeful and striking design.

From crafting distinctive logos that define a brand's identity, to designing heartfelt funeral and church programmes that honour special moments — I approach every project with professionalism, creativity, and genuine care.

My work spans across logo design, brand identity, event programmes, burial & memorial programmes, and print materials — each delivered with precision and a personal touch.

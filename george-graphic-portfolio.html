About Me

PASSION FOR DESIGN

I'm George Zirra, a creative graphic designer operating under the brand George Best Designs. I bring visual ideas to life with purpose, precision, and style.

From crafting distinctive logos that define a brand's identity, to designing heartfelt funeral and church programmes — I approach every project with professionalism and genuine care.

My work spans logo design, brand identity, church programmes, burial & memorial programmes, and print design — each delivered to the highest standard.
